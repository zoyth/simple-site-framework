# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how [Your Company] ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our email marketing platform and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Services.

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Name and contact information**: Full name, email address, phone number
- **Company information**: Company name, size, industry
- **Authentication data**: Password (encrypted), two-factor authentication settings
- **Billing information**: Credit card details, billing address (processed securely via Stripe)

### 1.2 Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you use our Services:

- **Activity logs**: Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Device information**: Browser type, operating system, IP address
- **Email campaign data**: Send volumes, open rates, click rates
- **API usage**: API calls, webhooks, integrations

### 1.3 Email Contact Data

When you use our Services to send emails, we process:

- **Contact lists**: Email addresses, names, custom fields
- **Email content**: Subject lines, body content, attachments
- **Engagement metrics**: Opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes

### 1.4 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Maintain your logged-in session
- Remember your preferences
- Analyze platform usage
- Improve our Services

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### 2.1 Service Provision

- **Account management**: Create and maintain your account
- **Email delivery**: Send email campaigns on your behalf
- **Platform features**: Provide analytics, automation, templates
- **Customer support**: Respond to inquiries and resolve issues

### 2.2 Service Improvement

- **Analytics**: Understand how users interact with our platform
- **Product development**: Develop new features and improvements
- **Performance optimization**: Monitor and improve platform reliability
- **Security**: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats

### 2.3 Communication

- **Service updates**: Notify you of platform changes, new features
- **Billing notifications**: Send invoices, payment confirmations
- **Marketing**: Send promotional emails (you can opt out anytime)
- **Legal notices**: Communicate policy changes, legal requirements

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

### 3.1 Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our Services:

- **Infrastructure**: AWS (hosting), Cloudflare (CDN)
- **Payment processing**: Stripe (credit card processing)
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics, Mixpanel
- **Customer support**: Zendesk, Intercom
- **Email delivery**: SendGrid, Amazon SES (infrastructure)

These providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

### 3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

- **Court orders or subpoenas**: Valid legal process
- **Law enforcement**: Legitimate requests from government authorities
- **Legal protection**: Enforce our Terms of Service, protect our rights
- **Safety**: Prevent harm to individuals or the public

### 3.3 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

### 3.4 With Your Consent

We may share your information for purposes not described in this policy with your explicit consent.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

- **Active accounts**: Data retained while account is active
- **Deleted accounts**: Data permanently deleted within 30 days of account closure
- **Legal requirements**: Some data retained longer if required by law
- **Backups**: Backup copies deleted within 90 days

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access controls**: Role-based access, two-factor authentication
- **Infrastructure security**: Secure data centers, regular security audits
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring and incident response
- **Employee training**: Regular security awareness training

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
