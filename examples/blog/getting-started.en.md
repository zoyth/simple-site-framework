# Getting Started with Our Platform

Welcome to our platform. Whether you are a small business owner or part of a larger team, this guide will walk you through everything you need to get up and running quickly.

## Creating Your Account

Getting started takes less than two minutes. Head to our sign-up page and enter your email address, company name, and a secure password. You will receive a verification email within seconds.

### Choosing the Right Plan

We offer three plans to fit different needs:

- **Starter** — Perfect for individuals and small teams. Includes core features and up to 3 projects.
- **Professional** — For growing businesses. Adds advanced analytics, team collaboration, and priority support.
- **Enterprise** — Custom solutions for large organizations with dedicated account management.

Not sure which plan fits your needs? Start with a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan. No credit card required.

## Setting Up Your First Project

Once your account is verified, you will land on the dashboard. Follow these steps to create your first project.

### Step 1: Define Your Goals

Click **New Project** and give it a descriptive name. Then select the primary goal for your project:

1. Lead generation
2. Customer engagement
3. Brand awareness
4. Sales conversion

This helps us tailor the default settings and recommend the best strategies for your use case.

### Step 2: Connect Your Data Sources

Our platform integrates with the tools you already use:

- **CRM systems** — Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive
- **Analytics** — Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude
- **Communication** — Slack, Microsoft Teams, email
- **E-commerce** — Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe

Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** to connect your accounts. Each integration includes a step-by-step setup wizard.

### Step 3: Configure Your Audience

Upload your contact list or let our platform discover your audience automatically. We support CSV imports, API syncing, and manual entry.

> **Tip:** Use tags to segment your audience from the start. Well-organized segments make targeting much more effective later on.

## Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard is your command center. Here is a quick overview of the main sections.

### Activity Feed

The activity feed shows real-time events across all your projects. You can filter by project, event type, or date range. Click any event to see the full details.

### Analytics Overview

The analytics panel displays key metrics at a glance:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Active Users | Unique users engaging with your content this period |
| Conversion Rate | Percentage of visitors who complete a goal action |
| Engagement Score | Composite score based on opens, clicks, and responses |
| Revenue Impact | Estimated revenue attributed to platform activities |

### Quick Actions

The sidebar provides shortcuts to common tasks:

- Create a campaign
- Import contacts
- Generate a report
- Invite a team member

## Best Practices

Based on our experience working with thousands of customers, here are some recommendations to help you get the most out of the platform.

### Start Small, Scale Gradually

Resist the urge to set up everything at once. Begin with one project and one integration. Learn the workflow, review your first results, and then expand. Teams that start small consistently see better long-term outcomes.

### Use Templates

We provide dozens of pre-built templates for campaigns, reports, and automations. Templates save time and incorporate industry best practices. Find them under **Resources > Templates**.

### Review Your Metrics Weekly

Set a weekly reminder to check your analytics dashboard. Look for trends rather than daily fluctuations. Our weekly summary email highlights the most important changes automatically.
